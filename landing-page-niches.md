# Khat Tourism: which 3–4 landing pages to build (Google Keyword Planner, Oct 2026)

**How this was worked out**
- Searches are Keyword Planner's 12-month averages. Close variants are counted once, and job, hotel, "for sale" and Safari Park searches are left out.
- "Abroad" means the UK, US, Canada, Australia, Germany and France combined. These are the pre-trip planners most likely to pay premium prices.
- Bid range is Google's top-of-page bid estimate in USD (low = 20th percentile, high = 80th). It shows what advertisers are already paying.
- **Break-even CPC** is the most Khat can pay per click and still make money:

  `booking value × Khat's margin × 3% click-to-booking rate`

  A niche is only profitable *for Khat* if this beats the bids. High bids only prove that *someone* profits, usually operators who own their own fleet.
- Margins are assumptions: **45%** on tours Khat runs itself, **15–25%** on resold activities. Swap in real numbers once conversion tracking works (see the audit).

## Recommended pages

### 1. Private Yacht Rental Dubai: `/yacht-rental-dubai`. Build first.
- **Demand:** the largest profitable pool, about 35–40k non-brand searches a month in the UAE plus 11k abroad, with medium competition. Khat already sells a private yacht at AED 800/hr.
- **Main keywords:**

  | Keyword | Searches/mo | Top bid |
  |---|---|---|
  | yacht rental dubai | 14,800 | $3.81 |
  | yacht dubai | 2,900 | $4.10 |
  | yacht party dubai | 2,400 | $1.73 |
  | yacht trip / yacht tour dubai | 1,900 | $3.23 |
  | yacht dubai marina | 1,300 | $3.72 |
  | yacht charter dubai | 1,000 | $4.91 |

- **Page:**
  - boats by size and number of guests, with real photos
  - price per hour and the minimum hours
  - what's included (crew, fuel, drinks)
  - occasions: birthday, proposal, sunset, family
  - add-ons: BBQ, cake and decorations, **jet ski add-on**
  - check availability on WhatsApp
- **Making it profit more:** push 3-hour bookings and add-ons. Every extra hour adds about $1.60 of break-even headroom per click.
- **Negative keywords:**
  - competitor names: xclusive, my cruises
  - for sale, jobs, club, show, captain, license

### 2. Sunrise Hot Air Balloon Dubai: `/hot-air-balloon-dubai`
- **Demand:** the best ratio of clicks to booking value. Clicks cost $0.21–1.75 for a booking worth AED 2,000+ per couple. About 6k non-brand searches a month in the UAE plus 2.4k abroad.
- **Main keywords:**

  | Keyword | Searches/mo | Top bid |
  |---|---|---|
  | hot air balloon dubai | 2,400 | $1.36 |
  | balloon ride dubai | 1,600 | $3.81 |
  | hot air balloon dubai price | 210 | $0.84 |

- **Needs:** a partnership with a licensed balloon operator, since Khat would be reselling.
- **Before bidding on "the dubai balloon"** (3,600 searches), check what it refers to. It may be a different, branded attraction.
- **Page:** hotel pickup, sunrise flight, breakfast, falcon photo.
- **Upsell:** "Balloon + Private Morning Desert Safari / Quad". The add-on is a tour Khat runs itself, so the margin on the bundle goes up.

### 3. Abu Dhabi Desert Safari + Day Trip from Dubai: `/abu-dhabi-desert-safari`
- **Demand:** about 21k safari searches a month plus about 8.7k Abu Dhabi city tour and Grand Mosque searches. Khat already runs 4 Abu Dhabi safaris and the mosque/city tours.
- **Main keywords:**

  | Keyword | Searches/mo | Top bid |
  |---|---|---|
  | abu dhabi desert safari | 14,800 | $3.85 |
  | abu dhabi desert safari tours | 1,300 | $3.85 |
  | abu dhabi city tour | 1,900 | $1.56 |
  | abu dhabi trip / city tour from dubai | 880 | $1.98 |
  | abu dhabi tour from dubai | 720 | $3.22 |
  | abu dhabi day trip from dubai | 210 | $2.85 |

- **Problem:** a single safari at AED 750 only just breaks even.
- **How to make it work:**
  - target people *in Abu Dhabi* with pickup from Abu Dhabi hotels
  - sell a **"Grand Mosque + city + desert safari" full-day combo from Dubai** at a higher price
  - add "cheap" as a negative keyword

### 4. Private / VIP Desert Safari Dubai: rebuild the core page
- **Demand:** the general term has the biggest volume, 40,500 searches a month, but at $1.64–5.45 a click the AED 750 safari is tight.
- **How to make it work:**
  - Bid first on **vip / premium / private / luxury desert safari dubai** (about 3k searches a month, mostly LOW competition).
  - Bid on the general terms only with ads that show the price up front, such as "Private 4x4 from AED 750, no shared buses".
  - Raise the booking value with add-ons: private dinner, quad or buggy upgrade, photographer.
- **Negatives:** cheap, deal, offer, groupon, price list.

### Only if they reprice: Dune buggy
- **What the bids show:** $2.32–6.65, up to $8.21 on "buggy desert dubai", the highest of any niche. Operators clearly make money here.
- **The problem:** at Khat's AED 450 price, every click loses money.
- **When it works:** if they sell per buggy, 1 hour, Polaris or Can-Am, at market price (AED 900–1,350), with a good partner rate. Break-even then rises to about $4 a click.

### Worth a small test: Private car with driver
- **Demand:** 7k searches a month in the UAE, bids $1.69–5.59, and up to $9.73 on "chauffeur service dubai". It fits a company that already has private vehicles and drivers.
- **Next step:** they need to tell us their hourly and day rates before we can score it.

## Next steps
1. Fix conversion tracking first (see the audit). Without it, none of these break-even numbers can be checked against real results.
2. Build pages 1 and 2. Both are new revenue with the best economics.
3. Upgrade pages 3 and 4. The tours already exist; they need landing pages built around these keywords.
4. Run each as its own campaign with a small budget for 3–4 weeks. Then replace the 3% and margin assumptions with real numbers and move budget accordingly.
