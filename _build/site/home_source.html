    Questions

    Are all your tours private?

    Yes. Every trip is just your group with your own driver or guide. No coaches and no strangers.

    How do I book?

    Send us your date and number of guests on WhatsApp, or call us. We confirm the price and pickup time, and you pay by card, bank transfer, PayPal or cash on the day.

    Where do you pick up from?

    From any hotel, hotel apartment or cruise terminal in Dubai. Abu Dhabi tours can pick up in Dubai or Abu Dhabi.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Cancel up to 48 hours before pickup for a full refund. If a sandstorm, heavy rain or a government weather warning stops the trip, you can move it to another day or get a full refund. See the cancellation policy.

    Can I pay in cash?

    Yes. You can pay by card, bank transfer, PayPal or in cash on the day.

    Do you have baby seats?

    Yes. Ask for a baby seat or booster when you book and tell us your children's ages.
