          Good to know

          • Dune bashing is bumpy. Tell us when you book if anyone is pregnant or has back, neck or heart problems, and we'll plan the trip around them.
          • Wear light, loose clothes and closed shoes. Bring sunglasses, and a light layer for winter evenings.
          • If a sandstorm, heavy rain or a government weather warning stops the trip, you can move it to another day or get a full refund.
          • Refunds go back to the way you paid within 3 to 5 working days. See the cancellation policy.

          Questions

          Which desert safari should I book?

          Pick the private evening safari to have the Land Cruiser to yourselves, the kids-friendly safari if you're bringing babies or young children, the candlelight dinner for a couple's evening, or the morning safari to avoid the afternoon heat.

          Will we share the car with other people?

          No. Every safari is private: one Land Cruiser and driver for your group only, and the price is for the whole car.

          Can we bring a baby or toddler?

          Yes. Ask for a baby seat or booster when you book, and tell us your children's ages so the driver can keep the dune bashing gentle.

          Where do you pick up from?

          Any hotel, hotel apartment or cruise terminal in Dubai.

          Can we add a quad bike or dune buggy?

          Yes, on most trips. Quad bikes and buggies are paid extras. Ask in your WhatsApp message and we'll add the price to your quote.

          When do I pay?

          Book online and pay by card, or confirm on WhatsApp and pay by bank transfer, PayPal or cash on the day.
