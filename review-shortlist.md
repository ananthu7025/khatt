# Tripadvisor review shortlist, by tour

Read on 1 Nov 2026 from Khat's seven Tripadvisor tour listings and the main Khat Tours and Safaris page. Every pick is a 5-star review. They are already in the site (`REVIEWS` in `_build/site/build.py`). Each page shows its own tours' reviews first, and choosing a tour moves its reviews to the front.

**What Khat needs to do:** open each link and paste one or two sentences into that review's `text` field. Until then, each card shows "Read [name]'s review on Tripadvisor".

**How they were picked:** each review answers a worry people have before booking:
- Is it safe with small children?
- Are there car seats?
- Will they pick us up on time?
- Is it private?
- Is it worth the price?

Recent reviews were preferred, along with ones that name the driver.

## Two reviews Khat should act on

1. **Lizzy P, 1 star, Oct 2026 (Dubai Desert Safari).** Told card was fine, then the guide asked for cash. Also unexpected paid extras at the camp and a 20-second camel ride.
   - The new site says "Pay on the day by card, transfer, PayPal or cash".
   - Make sure every driver can take card, or change that line.
   - List the paid extras at the camp in "Not included".
2. **Silke, 3 stars, Feb 2026 (Abu Dhabi kids morning).** No proper child safety seat.
   - The site promises "baby seats and boosters on request".
   - Every kids booking needs the right seat for each child's age.

Both were left off the site on purpose. So was a 5-star review titled "crazy driver", because that title would worry families.
